# Carlinho Physique  

**Carlinho Physique** is an interactive web platform developed for simulating and visualizing fundamental concepts of physics. The project combines a modern dark-mode interface with real-time graphical rendering powered by HTML5 Canvas.  

---  

## Code Structure  

- `index.html`: Webpage structure and module control.  
- `style.css`: CSS styling and responsive layout.  
- `script.js`: Graphical simulation engine and physics calculations.  
- `README.md`: Project documentation.  

---  

## Features and Modules  

### 1. Classical Mechanics  
* **Projectile Motion**: Simulation of parabolic trajectories.  
* **Adjustable Parameters**: Launch angle, initial velocity, and local gravity (Earth, Moon, Mars, and Jupiter).  
* **Real-time Calculations**: Maximum range (R), maximum height (H), and time of flight (t).  

### 2. Thermodynamics & Fluids  
* **Kinetic Theory of Gases**: Visual simulation of particle motion and collisions inside a container.  
* **Adjustable Parameters**: Temperature (K) and container volume (L).  
* **Real-time Calculations**: Ideal pressure (PV = nRT) and mean kinetic energy of the particles (\(E_k\)).  

### 3. Electromagnetism & Optics  
* **Geometric Optics**: Simulation of light beam refraction and reflection (Snell's Law).  
* **Adjustable Parameters**: Angle of incidence (θ₁) and refractive indices of Medium 1 and Medium 2 (n₁ and n₂).  
* **Real-time Calculations**: Angle of refraction (θ₂), total internal reflection detection, and the speed of light in the medium.  

### 4. Waves & Acoustics  
* **Wave Mechanics**: Propagation of continuous transverse waves.  
* **Adjustable Parameters**: Frequency (f) and amplitude.  
* **Real-time Calculations**: Wavelength (λ) and period (T).  

### 5. Modern Physics & Relativity  
* **Special Relativity**: Einstein's time dilation thought experiment.  
* **Adjustable Parameters**: Speed as a percentage of the speed of light (c).  
* **Real-time Calculations**: Time comparison between Earth's clock and the spacecraft's clock, Lorentz factor (γ), and photon energy (E = hf).
